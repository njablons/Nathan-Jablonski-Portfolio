# Individual Research DfAM

A common Design for Additive Manufacturing guideline is wall thickness. Parts with walls that are too thin can be difficult to print and may become weak or break easily. Designers can improve the strength and printability of a part by making sure the walls are thick enough for the specific 3D-printing process being used. This matters because proper wall thickness can make a part stronger, more durable, and easier to manufacture.

## Individual Research: FDM

Layer adhesion is an important issue in FDM printing because each layer of melted plastic needs to bond properly with the layer below it. If the layers do not stick together well, the finished part can become weak and may split apart under stress. A designer can improve layer adhesion by using the correct printing temperature, reducing excessive cooling, or adjusting the print settings to create stronger bonds between layers.

Two things that I learned was how choosing the correct wall thickness can make a 3D-printed part stronger and more reliable. Also, I learned that proper temperature settings can improve the way each layer bonds together, which can increase the overall strength of an FDM-printed part.
