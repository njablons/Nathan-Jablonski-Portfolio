## Part 2: Decide

### 1. Homepage Identity
The homepage serves as the primary gateway to my engineering documentation for course instructors, peers, and potential employers. I structured the homepage to immediately outline what this portfolio contains (laboratory design entries for MEGR 2156), how to navigate through the assignments, and the technical standards governing the work. By prioritizing navigation clarity and project context over personal biography, a reader can evaluate my engineering process within seconds of landing on the page.

### 2. Intentional Customization
I customized the color scheme and header typography in `mkdocs.yml` to reflect a clean, high-contrast engineering design aesthetic rather than the default MkDocs blue. The default styling lacked clear visual hierarchy between top-level project categories and sub-assignments. The higher contrast theme improves visual legibility when reviewing technical diagrams and code snippets.

### 3. Documentation Standard
I commit to documenting every design decision with enough specificity, mathematical justification, and clarity that an engineer could reproduce my exact workflow without needing to contact me.
